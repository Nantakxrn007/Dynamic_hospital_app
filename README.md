Development of a Prototype Readiness Index and an Interactive Simulation System
to Assess Hospital Infrastructure Preparedness for the Aging Population

The objectives of this project are:
To support provincial administrators in strategic planning for improving their local public healthcare systems
To enhance the quality of life of elderly populations through healthcare system improvements
To facilitate effective planning of healthcare resources and workforce to adequately meet the needs of the aging population
